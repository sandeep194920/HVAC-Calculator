ASHRAE / Sporlan Engineering Guidelines Summary:
  • Suction Lines: Max ΔP equivalent to 2°F (1.1°C) saturation temp drop. Min velocity 500-750 fpm (horizontal) or 1000-1500 fpm (vertical risers) for oil return. Max velocity 4000 fpm to prevent noise.
  • Liquid Lines: Max ΔP equivalent to 1-2°F saturation drop (typically 3-5 psi) to prevent flashing prior to the expansion valve. Maximum recommended velocity is 300 fpm to prevent liquid hammer.
  • Discharge Lines: Max ΔP equivalent to 2°F saturation drop. Min velocity 500-750 fpm for oil entrainment; Max velocity 3000-3500 fpm to avoid noise/vibration.
